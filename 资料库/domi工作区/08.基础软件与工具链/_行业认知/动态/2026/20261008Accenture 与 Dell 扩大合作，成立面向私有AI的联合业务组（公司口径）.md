## 全文重要观点

Accenture 与 Dell Technologies 于2026年10月8日宣布设立 Accenture Dell Business Group，把超过20年的合作进一步组织化，以 Accenture 的行业、业务转型及全栈服务能力结合 Dell 的基础设施与服务，帮助企业在私有、混合及主权环境中部署和扩大AI应用。公告的重点是补充企业现有云策略，特别服务受严格监管行业，并将私有AI工厂、集成技术栈、智能体用例、开源／开放权重模型选择与路由结合交付。超过3,000人的培训属于未来计划；RHAELM 案例属于公司及客户引述，未提供量化结果。公告末尾明确合作收益存在不确定性，不能把成立业务组、人员培训和方案定位当成已兑现订单、收入或ROI。

### 论点1：设立专门业务组旨在把长期合作推进到规模化私有AI交付。
**主张：**双方成立 Accenture Dell Business Group，帮助企业在自身环境中扩大AI部署并现代化基础设施。
**相应论据：**公告首段写明合作已有20+年，2026年10月8日宣布设立业务组；目标包括更强数据控制、更可预测的经济性，以及承载企业级智能体AI工作负载的性能。
**相应论证：**作者将既有合作基础与新业务组的组织安排连接，认为联合交付能支持更大规模部署。这是合作公告对未来能力的判断，未披露业务组法律实体结构、资本投入、订单金额或可归因收入，不能据此认定成立了股权合资公司。

### 论点2：双方以业务转型和基础设施能力互补，追求可衡量的业务结果。
**主张：**客户应能更快部署AI、匹配业务优先级，并兼顾控制、成本和性能。
**相应论据：**Julie Sweet 的引述强调从AI意愿走向规模执行和可衡量结果，覆盖私有、混合及主权环境；Michael Dell 的引述强调把 Accenture 的战略和行业专长与 Dell 的AI基础设施及服务结合，用于简化业务流程和建立全栈能力。
**相应论证：**公司给出的因果链是行业和业务需求定义、技术基础设施、部署与服务共同作用，使AI进入实际流程。原文未提供部署周期缩短比例、成本降幅、性能基准或独立ROI测算，实际效果仍待验证。

### 论点3：私有AI交付面向多行业，并借助主权云和新型云供应商关系扩大覆盖。
**主张：**业务组希望把既有行业经验转化为私有基础设施上的AI部署能力。
**相应论据：**公告列举金融服务、生命科学、制造业、工业和高科技行业的既有合作经验，并称可利用与主权云及新型云供应商的关系开展部署。
**相应论证：**作者以历史行业经验和生态关系解释业务组的定位及渠道基础；未给出各行业客户数量、项目分布、云供应商名单或交付成功率。因此行业列表是覆盖范围说明，不能当成新增客户或已取得市场份额的证据。

### 论点4：RHAELM 案例为主权AI场景提供客户侧佐证，但不足以量化商业成效。
**主张：**双方合作能帮助主权云服务供应商加快安全、合规及高性能方案交付。
**相应论据：**当前官方正文称，双方正在帮助英国AI基础设施与解决方案供应商 RHAELM 加快其主权云方案交付；其 CEO兼CRO Bradd Lewis 表示双方能力支持客户以更快速度和更大规模部署主权AI。
**相应论证：**作者用特定客户及其负责人引述，说明基础设施和行业转型能力能共同服务主权AI场景。该案例未披露合同金额、部署数量、客户名单、验收结果或速度指标，属于公司及客户定性陈述，未经过独立效果核验。当前官网用“正在帮助／使客户能够”的措辞；搜索索引曾呈现更强的已部署措辞，归档以本次直接读取的官网正文为准。

### 论点5：培训计划用于扩充Dell产品组合交付能力，本地AI基础设施定位为云策略的补充。
**主张：**业务组通过人员能力建设，面向受严格监管行业提供综合解决方案。
**相应论据：**Accenture 计划培训超过3,000名从业人员，建立对 Dell 产品组合的深入能力；业务组将以本地AI基础设施补充企业云策略，特别面向高度监管行业。
**相应论证：**人员培训对应多产品整合与实施的能力需求，本地部署对应客户对数据、合规和运行环境的要求。超过3,000人是计划培训人数，未说明完成期限、已培训人数、专职编制、认证情况或生产交付能力；“补充云策略”也不意味着企业整体退出公有云。

### 论点6：私有AI与新型云方案以全栈AI工厂和全生命周期服务增强控制。
**主张：**可扩展且安全的AI工厂应改善数据、合规与性能控制。
**相应论据：**第一项方案由 Dell 端到端产品组合及生态伙伴构成全栈AI Factories，由 Dell 与 Accenture 提供全生命周期服务，覆盖私有AI与新型云方案。
**相应论证：**公司把硬件、软件生态及持续服务整合到同一交付范围，用以支撑控制和规模化运行；未披露具体物料清单、认证框架、运维SLA、收费模式或合规审计结果，不能把方案目标当成已经取得监管认证。

### 论点7：集成私有AI技术栈同时支持智能体AI、推理和高性能计算。
**主张：**联合方案覆盖多种企业工作负载，而不只是单一模型接入。
**相应论据：**第二项方案描述以 Dell 完整集成的解决方案，通过 Accenture 全栈产品与服务支持 agentic AI、inferencing 和 high-performance computing 工作负载。
**相应论证：**作者以集成基础设施与全栈交付的组合解释多工作负载支持能力；没有展示模型清单、推理吞吐、时延、GPU利用率、HPC基准或跨负载资源管理细节，因此只可确认其方案覆盖定位，不能判定性能领先或经济性更优。

### 论点8：智能体用例和加速器旨在缩短价值兑现时间。
**主张：**预设智能体用例及加速器可以加快规模化AI应用的业务价值兑现。
**相应论据：**第三项方案列出 agentic use cases 与 accelerators，并称有助于更快取得 scaled AI use cases 的 time-to-value。
**相应论证：**原文只给出产品与服务定位，没有具体用例、实施过程、对照案例或时间节省数据，未展开论证；不能补造标准化组件带来的效率提升，也不能将加速器理解为已明确型号的硬件加速卡。

### 论点9：开放模型与按任务路由是控制成本、数据及跨环境运行的一部分。
**主张：**在预定义私有基础设施栈上，模型选择和任务感知路由有助于维持成本可预测性、数据控制及可扩展运行。
**相应论据：**第四项方案明确涉及 open source 与 open-weight models，支持跨模型、跨环境的 model selection 与 task-aware routing。
**相应论证：**公司将任务和模型匹配、运行环境选择同成本及数据控制连接，但未公布路由规则、模型许可、评测集、成本结果或具体模型供应商。开源模型与开放权重模型并列列示，不能将开放权重一概等同于完整开源；此段未证明私有部署总成本低于其他部署方式。

### 论点10：Accenture 的集团规模是交付背景，不是本业务组的业绩。
**主张：**公司介绍以人才、客户和集团收入规模说明其端到端交付背景。
**相应论据：**公告“About Accenture”称集团约814,000名员工、约9,000家客户，FY26收入约740亿美元；并列示 Reinvention Services 及AI、数据、行业、流程和技术能力。
**相应论证：**这些数字描述 Accenture 集团，属于公告日公司介绍口径，原文并未将其拆分到AI业务或 Dell 联合业务组。不能把集团客户数、员工数或FY26收入当成该业务组的客户、编制或收入，也不能凭此计算合作新增市场规模。

### 论点11：合作收益和AI应用结果具有前瞻性风险，公告没有保证目标实现。

# **Accenture and Dell Technologies Expand Collaboration to Help Enterprises Scale Private AI and Modernize Infrastructure**

NEW YORK; Oct. 8, 2026 – [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com) (NYSE: ACN) and [Dell Technologies](https://www.dell.com/en-us) today announced a major expansion of their 20+ year collaboration with the formation of the Accenture Dell Business Group, designed to help organizations scale AI within their own environment and modernize their infrastructure. Together, the companies can give enterprises greater control over their data, more predictable economics, and the performance needed to run enterprise-grade agentic AI workloads.

“By expanding our collaboration with Dell, we’re strengthening the power of our organizations to help our clients move from AI ambition to scaled execution to measurable business outcomes," said Julie Sweet, chair and CEO, Accenture. "Enterprises will be able to deploy AI solutions faster, align them with their business priorities, maintain greater control through private, hybrid and sovereign environments, and optimize both cost and performance."

![](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/media_12b3d068134cfc82e5ab1441770cae655f3a20f8e.jpg?width=750&format=jpg&optimize=medium)

“Dell Technologies and Accenture bring a deep history of working side-by-side to help our customers unlock their biggest opportunities,” said Michael Dell, chairman and CEO, Dell Technologies. “The formation of a dedicated business group deepens that collaboration to help enterprises move from AI interest to adoption. By bringing together Accenture’s strategic and industry expertise with Dell’s AI-ready infrastructure and services, we’re helping customers streamline business processes and build the full-stack capabilities needed to deploy AI at scale.”

The Accenture Dell Business Group builds on a strong track record of helping clients in industries such as financial services, life sciences, manufacturing, industrials and high-tech use AI to transform their businesses. It is uniquely positioned to help organizations deploy AI on private infrastructure, leveraging relationships with sovereign and neo cloud providers.

For RHAELM, a UK-based AI infrastructure and solutions provider, Accenture and Dell are contributing to accelerating the delivery of its sovereign cloud solutions, focused on secure, compliant, high-performing operations.

“By leveraging Dell’s AI-ready infrastructure and services and Accenture’s deep industry and transformation expertise, we are enabling our clients to deploy cutting-edge sovereign AI at pace and scale,” said Bradd Lewis, CEO and CRO, RHAELM. “The Accenture Dell Business Group brings a deeper level of collaboration that directly benefits our clients.”

To support the Accenture Dell Business Group, Accenture plans to train more than 3,000 practitioners to build deep capability and expertise across the Dell portfolio. This group will deliver comprehensive solutions to help organizations complement their cloud strategies with on-premises AI infrastructure — particularly for those in highly regulated industries. Together, these capabilities provide a unified approach to accelerating AI adoption while aligning technology investments to business outcomes:

- **Private AI and neocloud solutions**: Designed to help organizations enhance control over data, compliance, and performance through scalable and secure AI Factories built with full-stack solutions consisting of Dell’s end-to-end portfolio and ecosystem partners, supported by full lifecycle services from Dell and Accenture.

- **Integrated private AI stack offerings**: Enterprise AI infrastructure brings together Dell’s complete, fully integrated solutions to support agentic AI, inferencing and high-performance computing workloads through Accenture’s full-stack offerings.

- **Accelerated agentic solutions deployment:** Agentic use cases and accelerators help drive faster time-to-value from scaled AI use cases.

- **Open source and open-weight models:** Designed to support model selection and task‑aware routing across models and environments, helping to maintain cost predictability, data control and scalable AI operations on a pre-defined private infrastructure stack.

Learn more about the Accenture and Dell Technologies collaboration [here](https://accenture.com/dell).

# **Accenture and Dell Technologies Expand Collaboration to Help Enterprises Scale Private AI and Modernize Infrastructure**

NEW YORK; Oct. 8, 2026 – [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com) (NYSE: ACN) and [Dell Technologies](https://www.dell.com/en-us) today announced a major expansion of their 20+ year collaboration with the formation of the Accenture Dell Business Group, designed to help organizations scale AI within their own environment and modernize their infrastructure. Together, the companies can give enterprises greater control over their data, more predictable economics, and the performance needed to run enterprise-grade agentic AI workloads.

“By expanding our collaboration with Dell, we’re strengthening the power of our organizations to help our clients move from AI ambition to scaled execution to measurable business outcomes," said Julie Sweet, chair and CEO, Accenture. "Enterprises will be able to deploy AI solutions faster, align them with their business priorities, maintain greater control through private, hybrid and sovereign environments, and optimize both cost and performance."

![](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2026/media_12b3d068134cfc82e5ab1441770cae655f3a20f8e.jpg?width=750&format=jpg&optimize=medium)

“Dell Technologies and Accenture bring a deep history of working side-by-side to help our customers unlock their biggest opportunities,” said Michael Dell, chairman and CEO, Dell Technologies. “The formation of a dedicated business group deepens that collaboration to help enterprises move from AI interest to adoption. By bringing together Accenture’s strategic and industry expertise with Dell’s AI-ready infrastructure and services, we’re helping customers streamline business processes and build the full-stack capabilities needed to deploy AI at scale.”

The Accenture Dell Business Group builds on a strong track record of helping clients in industries such as financial services, life sciences, manufacturing, industrials and high-tech use AI to transform their businesses. It is uniquely positioned to help organizations deploy AI on private infrastructure, leveraging relationships with sovereign and neo cloud providers.

For RHAELM, a UK-based AI infrastructure and solutions provider, Accenture and Dell are contributing to accelerating the delivery of its sovereign cloud solutions, focused on secure, compliant, high-performing operations.

“By leveraging Dell’s AI-ready infrastructure and services and Accenture’s deep industry and transformation expertise, we are enabling our clients to deploy cutting-edge sovereign AI at pace and scale,” said Bradd Lewis, CEO and CRO, RHAELM. “The Accenture Dell Business Group brings a deeper level of collaboration that directly benefits our clients.”

To support the Accenture Dell Business Group, Accenture plans to train more than 3,000 practitioners to build deep capability and expertise across the Dell portfolio. This group will deliver comprehensive solutions to help organizations complement their cloud strategies with on-premises AI infrastructure — particularly for those in highly regulated industries. Together, these capabilities provide a unified approach to accelerating AI adoption while aligning technology investments to business outcomes:

- **Private AI and neocloud solutions**: Designed to help organizations enhance control over data, compliance, and performance through scalable and secure AI Factories built with full-stack solutions consisting of Dell’s end-to-end portfolio and ecosystem partners, supported by full lifecycle services from Dell and Accenture.

- **Integrated private AI stack offerings**: Enterprise AI infrastructure brings together Dell’s complete, fully integrated solutions to support agentic AI, inferencing and high-performance computing workloads through Accenture’s full-stack offerings.

- **Accelerated agentic solutions deployment:** Agentic use cases and accelerators help drive faster time-to-value from scaled AI use cases.

- **Open source and open-weight models:** Designed to support model selection and task‑aware routing across models and environments, helping to maintain cost predictability, data control and scalable AI operations on a pre-defined private infrastructure stack.

Learn more about the Accenture and Dell Technologies collaboration [here](https://accenture.com/dell).
